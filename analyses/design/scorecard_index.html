Brier score: the average squared gap between the stated probability and what happened (1 or 0). 0.25 is what always saying 50% scores; lower is better. "Always down" (40% up every day) is shown only as a warning: in a falling month it looks smart without any skill.

How this is worked out, and what it can't tell you yet

Calls come from the Desk Ledger and count in the week they resolve. "Desk, S&P" and "Formula" use only S&P calls where the plain VIX formula (zero drift, VIX-implied daily move) gives a probability, so both are graded on the same calls. "Follow yesterday" leans 60% in the direction of the last finished session before each up/down call; 60/40 was fixed in advance, not fitted. The VIX formula always says 50% on up/down, so it scores 0.25 there.

Caveats: several calls often resolve on the same day, so 22 up/down calls are really about a dozen independent outcomes. The S&P fell on most days in this stretch, which flatters any rule that leans down. Simulator grades and the paper book start Fri 27 Nov.
