How to read this

  • Real: Friday's close, Thursday's low, VIX 25.64, the 200-day average (7,325, computed from a year of FMP closes) and every mechanism and date above, taken from your reports.
  • Assumed: the odds of each catalyst outcome (CPI 30/45/25, CoreWeave 50/50, forced selling 50/50), how far each one moves fair value, and how much each group trades. The odds are mine, not the market's.
  • Good for: seeing which question matters most, how forced flows and the 200-day trigger shape the path, and who absorbs the selling. It does not say which path the week takes.
  • Left out: Nvidia (17 Nov), the data-centre loan auction (19 Nov), the FOMC (9 Dec), and the January legislatures your Proactive Report ranks above all of these.
