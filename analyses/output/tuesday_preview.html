What drives it: housing costs (owners' equivalent rent) are expected to cool from 0.35% to about 0.27%. Gasoline fell about 1% in October. Airfares should give back September's +1.2%. Core goods are roughly flat, with little tariff pass-through so far. Oil's 13% fall last week lands in November's numbers, not October's.

How each print reads: 0.3%+ core matches ING's outlier and pushes the December hike toward fully priced. 0.2% is "no change", per Citi, and attention moves to Friday's PPI. 0.1% or less is the "significant downside" one strategist says could stop full pricing. December hike odds are about 77% going in. No source gave an implied S&P move for CPI day itself.

Corrections to earlier reports

  • CoreWeave's backlog is $104.2bn (30 Jun 2026), not $55.6bn. $55.6bn was the Q3 2025 figure.
  • CoreWeave's debt is $35.6bn per the June 10-Q. The $50bn+ figures use a wider definition.
  • "Most of the 2.7GW is in Texas" is not supported. The letter names six Texas sites but gives no split.
  • No year-on-year CPI will be published this month.
  • Nvidia reports Tuesday 17 Nov. The Weekend Update listed it as Monday.
