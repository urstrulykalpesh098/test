# Self-research report, data as of 2026-11-24

In-sample = before 2022-01-01, out-of-sample = 2022-01-01 on. Proposals only; nothing changes without Rk.

## rule5_spx_1.6x: REJECT

Belief: 80% S&P ranges should be 1.6x the VIX-implied move (rule 5, lesson L3).

Over years of data a 1.6x range held 98% of the time since 2022, far more than 80%. It is right for a shock month like November 2026 but far too wide in normal times. Proposal: size ranges from recent realized volatility (the adaptive band) instead of a fixed 1.6x.

## rule5_vix_25pct: REJECT

Belief: 80% VIX bands should be +/-25% of the prior close for one day (rule 5).

A +/-25% one-day VIX band held 99% of days since 2022. Too wide in normal markets; the adaptive band keeps close to 80% and still widens in a storm.

## adaptive_spx_band: ADOPT

Belief: Size 80% S&P ranges from half VIX + half recent realized vol, width fitted before 2022.

Fitted on 2016-2021, it held 77% from 2022 on, close to the 80% target. But it held only 53% in the last 15 days, so in a shock it still runs narrow. Proposal: use the wider of the adaptive band and the VIX-implied band once VIX is above 25.

## lesson_L4_falling_tape: KEEP WATCHING

Belief: Don't default to 'up' after a falling week (lesson L4).

After a week down 2%+, the next day was up 52% of the time since 2022 (normal 53%). Historically falling weeks did NOT make up days rarer, so L4 as a rule of thumb is not supported by the long record; it may still be right in a specific regime.

## vix_curve_inverted: ADOPT

Belief: When 3-month VIX is below VIX (inverted curve), the next week moves closer to what VIX prices.

When the VIX curve inverts, the following week realizes much more of the VIX-implied move than usual, in both halves of history. Proposal: when inverted, do not shrink ranges below the VIX-implied move.

## setup_analogs_5d: REJECT

Belief: The setup playbook's 'P(up in 5 days)' from look-alike days beats the plain up-rate.

Tested since 2022 on 245 days using only earlier history: analogs scored 0.245 vs 0.244 for the plain up-rate (lower is better). They do not beat the base rate, so treat the playbook as a picture of the range of outcomes, not as a forecast.

## specialists_beat_base: REJECT

Belief: At least one specialist analyst beats the plain up-rate on next-day calls.

Best analyst (rates) scored 0.2481 vs 0.2484 for the plain up-rate across ~1,950 out-of-sample days. A gap that small is noise: none of the simple signals predicts tomorrow's direction.

Not investment advice; analysis only.
