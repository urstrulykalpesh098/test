How to read this

  • What is real: Friday's close (7,376.86), Thursday's low (7,350.35), the 200-day average (, computed from a year of FMP closes), VIX 25.64, and the cast of traders and mechanisms from your reports.
  • What is assumed: the three weekend paths, how big each shock is, and how much each group trades. Volatility-target funds are sized to sell about 3–11% of the day's volume, depending on the path.
  • What it is good for: seeing how a day unfolds, meaning who absorbs the selling, when forced flows hit and how much they add. It does not say which path Monday takes.
  • Why not MiroFish: MiroFish simulates people posting on social media. Prices move when traders place orders, so this runs our own order-book engine with trader agents. It runs entirely inside the session, so nothing goes to MiroFish's makers.
